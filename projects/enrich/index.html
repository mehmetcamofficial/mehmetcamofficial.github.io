ENRICH Triage Engine

Interactive triage-engine prototype demonstrating an applied decision-support workflow.

Decision-support concept

ENRICH Triage Engine is an interactive prototype for exploring structured triage and decision-support workflows. The portfolio uses it as an example of applying software and AI concepts to a bounded decision process rather than presenting it as a finished, clinically approved service.

How to assess the prototype

Visitors can explore the linked demonstration and consider how inputs, rules and outputs are presented. The portfolio does not publish independent clinical validation, deployment metrics or guaranteed decision accuracy. No real-world medical decision should rely on an unvalidated prototype.

Related portfolio directions

OncoConnect addresses connected digital experiences, while the histopathology AI project explores image-analysis ideas. The applied AI research page provides broader context for evaluation, human oversight and reliable software development.
