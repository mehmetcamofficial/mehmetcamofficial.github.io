Evidence and boundaries

The available portfolio material does not establish that OncoConnect is an approved medical device, a diagnostic service or a source of personalized clinical treatment. This page therefore makes no claim about medical effectiveness, patient outcomes or production-scale adoption. It is a product reference rather than medical guidance.
