TourPilot

TourPilot is an AI-assisted operations platform designed to turn fragmented tour-operator workflows into a more structured, auditable and production-minded system.

Problem context

Operational data arrives from multiple sources and must be normalized without silently losing booking, passenger or assignment context.

Engineering approach

Incremental delivery, server-side RBAC, idempotency, duplicate prevention, staging separation and human review for ambiguous records.

Core workflow

Reservation ingestion → validation/reconciliation → operational planning → assignments → audit trail.

Technology signals

TypeScript, PostgreSQL/Neon, Render, automation workflows and AI-assisted engineering.

What the project demonstrates

  • Designing software around real operational constraints rather than a static portfolio mockup.
  • Handling messy reservation data with explicit review states instead of unsafe guessing.
  • Building authorization and auditability into operational workflows.
  • Using AI-assisted development while retaining deterministic business rules and human-in-the-loop controls.

Engineering principles

The documented development approach emphasizes Kaizen and Clean Code, staging-first validation, destructive-migration avoidance, idempotent processing and duplicate prevention. Ambiguous operational data is routed to review rather than silently merged.

Evidence boundary
