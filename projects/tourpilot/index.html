TourPilot

AI-assisted operations platform built around real tour-operator workflows, auditability and production-minded data handling.

Operational scope

TourPilot is presented as an AI-assisted platform for tour-operator workflows. The portfolio highlights reservation ingestion, operational planning, data quality, role-based access control (RBAC), auditability and automation. These are documented areas of the product, not claims about user counts, commercial scale or performance.

Engineering approach

The published engineering approach emphasizes incremental delivery, Kaizen and Clean Code. It also calls out duplicate prevention, idempotent processing, separation of staging and production, and human review for important decisions. Together these principles describe how the project approaches operational reliability and traceable changes.
