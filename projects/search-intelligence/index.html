Search Intelligence

Interactive application focused on search intelligence and AI-assisted analysis.

What the application explores

Search Intelligence is an interactive application focused on search relevance and AI-assisted analysis. The related engineering article documents the progression from machine-learning portfolio experiments into search retrieval, ranking evaluation and a Streamlit-based experience.

Engineering evidence

The published project narrative discusses retrieval approaches, candidate ranking, benchmark-driven evaluation and the importance of traceable decisions. Its emphasis is on measuring changes rather than treating a persuasive AI response as sufficient evidence. This page summarizes that direction and points to the more detailed article for methods and caveats.

Current scope

The original article explicitly describes ongoing work and distinguishes research-stage measurements from production acceptance. The linked application can be explored, but this portfolio entry does not imply that all experimental components are fully deployed.
